Abstract

A genealogy of the prophetic lineage (Adam → Noah → Ibrahim → … → Muhammad ﷺ) and of the family of Imam Hussain ibn Ali at Karbala: his wives, his children, and those who killed them. The page opens on the graph alone; press back for this article. Hover a name for who they were; click the graph to fit it to the page.

We drew it as an Org list, by org-special-block-extras: each item is a person, nesting is descent, and a template such as :married: styles an arc —all without a single line of raw dot.
