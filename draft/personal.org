** Share my weekly review with Montather, as a way to connect with him /and/ as a way to keep myself accountable
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: share-my-weekly-review-with-montather-as-a-way-to-connect-with-him-and-as-a-way-to-keep-myself-accountable
:END:

i.e., summarise my weekly review into a paragraph and send that to him.

*Bring a buddy*

GTD is a very lonely habit. It is only you and your productivity and at
times it can feel like you are doing things in a vacuum. Find a GTD pal
or a Weekly Review Buddy and nudge them for influence or tips. We at
Arkus sometimes do our Weekly Reviews together.

*Socialize it*

If buddy isn't working, shout it out to the world. Post it on social
media or internal groups. I tweet my Weekly Review every week as well as
post it internally in our GTD Slack Channel.
