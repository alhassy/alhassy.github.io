Deferred until a few repos are migrated.  Each of these serves its own copy of a write-up at
=alhassy.com/<repo>/=, which is precisely what a :REDIRECT: heading here replaces; one
canonical URL per write-up is the point.

- [ ] =holy-books= ---=alhassy.com/holy-books/= ↦ =alhassy.com/holy-books-in-org-mode=
- [X] =org-special-block-extras= ---Pages off; its blog slug is its repo name, so old links still land.
- [ ] =lf=
- [ ] =emacs.d=
- [ ] =del= ---just a “Hello, world”; probably axe the repo outright.

Per repo:
1. Swap its =index.html= for a one-line =<meta http-equiv="refresh" content="0; url=…">=, so
   old links ---Reddit threads, MELPA's homepage link--- land on the blog post rather than a 404.
   (Disabling Pages outright breaks those links.)
2. Axe what only the standalone page needed: =#+EXPORT_FILE_NAME: index=, and any =blog:header=
   ---that link type is gone; kbd:M-x_my/blogging-mode styles any Org file.
   (=blog--prepare-redirect= strips =#+export_file_name:= anyway, so that's tidiness.)
3. Point its "full article as HTML" badges at the blog post.

Cheat sheets keep their Pages: those serve PDFs, not write-ups.
